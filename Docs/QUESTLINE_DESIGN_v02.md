# VIGIL & Luminant — The Forgotten Bond (story design v0.2)

## Canon rules
- One VIGIL sword, one living male bearer, one living female Luminant mantle.
- The Lumen is not a hereditary xenogene. Its succession depends on world state.
- Neither character is owned by the other. The Knight's Oath is chosen, not forced.
- VIGIL's personality is the **Demanding Mentor**: stern, protective, willing to challenge and ultimately willing to concede error.
- The first encounters should not require the separate Heralds of Horax mod. That project is explicitly shelved.

## Secret at the heart of the mod
The Concordance did not create the Lumen; it discovered the phenomenon and failed to reproduce it. VIGIL's original third directive was to **judge whether the Luminant herself should be contained or killed if her power became dangerous**. The sword initially remembers only its protective directives. As memory fragments are recovered, the chosen bearer must decide whether to accept or reject the command. The Luminant must have agency in the ultimate resolution.

## Story progression
### Act I. The Silent Vault
- PROTOTYPE implemented only as a sword appearing on the player's home map after day 15. Actual ancient vault map / quest not yet coded.
- VIGIL is a white-and-gold blade whose broken halo encloses an inactive prism.
- Male colonists can petition VIGIL. This provisional alpha bonding step will later become the Three Witnesses.

### Act II. A Light Returns
- Only after the world registry finds an eligible female newborn, she receives the 'luminant' trait and a latent lumen health condition.
- The trait remains visible on the Bio tab; the Health condition displays awakening stages.
- Gradual Awakening: at most one small defensive burst per 2 days during childhood; it does not prevent death.
- At biological age 7 a 'First Light' letter plays, introducing her Luminist psycasting path.

### Act III. The Oath
- When a male bearer and the Luminant meet, the bearer may voluntarily swear guardianship.
- The sword feels confused recognition, as if the girl were reflected in its oldest memory.
- New future mechanics: limited emergency intercession, a Luminous Blade infusion, and paired Resonance generation.

### Act IV. The Archive of Ash
- Intended quest: a small ruin with an obsolete civilization-era record core. Not implemented.
- Records reveal that the Concordance studied earlier Luminants but concealed the failures.
- The party encounters ambiguous reports describing VIGIL as both guardian and executioner.

### Act V. The Third Directive
- Intended late-game dilemma: the sword receives a sealed order to judge the Luminant as a potential archotech threat.
- Divergence: obey a narrow mechanistic order; defy it on principle; reconcile it through proof of human judgment.
- No storyline is to force the Luminant's death; the player's choice and in-world actions matter.

### Finale. True Accord
- The sword's halo completes in a temporary battle effect and its executioner contingency is voluntarily rewritten.
- The Luminant gains Dawnbringer, while the knight gains the paired Last Dawn ability.
- Paths emphasize preservation, finality, or sovereignty; each must be balanced.

## Relationships and succession
- The bonded Knight can be a father, brother, partner, former raider, or stranger. Relationships are emergent RimWorld stories.
- Death of the Luminant initiates a one-year mantle cooldown; later births can inherit it.
- Resurrection before replacement restores the same identity. Resurrection after replacement does not create two Luminants.
- The sword mourns its bearer and can ultimately choose a successor; that succession trial is still to be implemented.
