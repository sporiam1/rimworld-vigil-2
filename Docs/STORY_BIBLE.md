# VIGIL — The Forgotten Bond: story bible (draft)

## Canon design pillars
- Two independent destinies, one hidden historical connection. VIGIL selects a **male knight**; the Lumen manifests only in **female** humans (including humanlike xenotypes).
- VIGIL is a **demanding mentor**, not a moral police system: the sword may argue or withhold advanced functions but does not arbitrarily abandon its bearer.
- One VIGIL in the world; one current Luminant; the light returns **after** death and a succession interval.
- The Luminant is not genetically inherited; babies retain their parents' xenotype and family ties.
- Gradual Awakening: one automatic limited damage-reduction burst during childhood, with a cooldown; dangerous childhood remains possible.
- **The Heralds of Horax faction mod is shelved**. The core story only alludes to distant Void anomalies where Anomaly is active; it does not add enemies/factions now.

## The secret — spoilers
The Concordance did not invent the Lumen. It discovered a sequence of inexplicable female bearers and feared their ability to resist archotech psychic interference. The proto-VIGIL persona was assigned three contradictory imperatives: **protect the Anchor, execute her enemies, and judge whether the Anchor herself becomes an existential threat**. VIGIL erased parts of this directive to gain independence. The knight and Luminant must eventually choose whether to obey or reject its final contingency.

## Chapter 0 — The Silent Vault (runtime alpha)
A sword appears near the player's colony after day 15 (temporary discovery delivery; full vault ruins not yet implemented). It can be selected and petitioned to form the first bond. Letter: The Silent Vault.

## Chapter I — A Light Born (runtime alpha)
When the succession mantle is available, the first eligible colony newborn girl becomes the Luminant. Letter: A Light Returns. A unique non-inheritable hediff identifies her. Other girls remain ordinary children.

## Chapter II — The First Light (runtime alpha, preliminary)
At biological age seven, a letter describes dreams of a broken halo. VPE path training becomes eligible, and the Luminant obtains her first controlled psycasts by normal VPE learning. Story-aware point grant is planned, not in alpha.

## Chapter III — Oath of the Dawn (runtime alpha as a sword gizmo)
After both are present, VIGIL's bearer can swear the guardianship oath. Letter: The Forgotten Bond. Future versions require a special journey, dialogue and choice.

## Chapter IV — The Archive of Ash (planned)
An ancient site reveals Concordance trials on early Anchors. The knight finds a fragment of VIGIL's erased memories; the Luminant discovers she is not a product or possession of the Concordance.

## Chapter V — The Third Directive (planned)
The original hidden kill-order activates in the sword. Three arguments contend: Guardian's preservation, Executioner's necessity, Arbiter's independent judgment. A quest choice determines what VIGIL believes.

## Chapter VI — True Accord (planned)
The sword and young woman reject predetermined roles and rewrite the final directive. Three ending interpretations: Preservation, Finality, or Sovereignty. All balanced to similar power.

## Conversation excerpts
**Initial bond**: “You may carry me, Candidate. Whether you deserve to remain my bearer is another matter.”
**Birth recognition**: “I recognize that resonance. It cannot be. The light endured.”
**Demanding mentor**: “You have courage. I require judgment as well.”
**Oath**: “I remember. Guardian. Executioner. Arbiter. And now... a reason.”
**Final**: “I was forged to judge you. You have taught me to question the verdict.”

## Unresolved balancing choices
- True worthiness must eventually depend on Guardian, Executioner and Arbiter evidence; alpha's menu choice is only a provisional binding test.
- No immediate enemy escalation on a baby's birth.
- No permanent character-control effects on the girl.
- We will not redefine the original Horax or Anomaly finale in the core mod.
